01. cold-open
A customer clicks once. The webhook fires twice. Two orders. Inventory counted down twice.

02. title
This is Reflex. Every fix your team verifies becomes training data for a model you own. Your agent, your model, your memory. One loop.

03. 01 · Reproduce
First, Reflex replays the incident. The handler runs in an OS sandbox against four behavior checks. Two fail. The retry isn't idempotent.

04. 02 · Repair
River writes a patch that catches the replay before inventory moves. Reflex runs it against the same contract. 4 of 4. One order. 15 seconds.

05. 03 · Verify
Nothing trains unless execution proves it. On 24 practice tasks, River's repairs passed every check on 19. Add 5 accepted fixes: 24 verified examples, each with provenance.

06. 04 · Train
Then we train our own model. Qwen3.5-9B, fine-tuned on River. 48 LoRA steps. 122K tokens. A checkpoint we own.

07. 05 · Reuse
Now the loop closes. Our UFO extension calls that checkpoint as a tool. It fixes the stock-replay bug: 2 of 4 → 4 of 4, in 5.7 seconds. And the trace flows back into Reflex as new experience.

08. 06 · Measure
We measure it like researchers. Held-out incidents never touch training. Memory and learned runs get identical inputs. All three pass 4 of 4. The benchmark saturated, so harder incidents are next.

09. 07 · Proof
240 tests pass. The Repository view already runs pytest against patches on local Python code. And every number here has a receipt in the repo.

10. close
Your failures become your dataset. Your dataset becomes your model. That's Reflex. Built solo at YC's Own Your Intelligence hackathon.
